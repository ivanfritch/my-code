About Ivan and Daryll

Ivan and Daryll are two college freshmen who became good friends while starting their college journey. College was a new experience for both of them, and having a friend made the journey more enjoyable.

They support each other with school activities, assignments, projects, and everyday challenges. Their friendship continues to grow through teamwork, laughter, and shared experiences.
